Safety comes first

Exploring is free. Meeting people takes one extra step: you verify your identity before you message, join or post a trip. This is what we're building:

  • Identity check with an ID and a selfie, done by a specialist provider.
  • Reviews from both sides, written after the trip.
  • A suggestion to hold the first meetup in a public place.
  • A safety button, free for everyone, always.
  • Reports, blocking and a team that reviews them.

No app can remove every risk. We design to lower it and we'll tell you plainly how it works.

Questions

Is Lumen Travels available yet?

Not yet. We're validating the idea and building it. Join the list and we'll write to you when it opens.

Where will it work first?

Colombia, along the Medellín – Bogotá – Caribbean route (Cartagena, Santa Marta, Tayrona). The app will be in English and Spanish.

Is it free?

The plan is a free base. A paid "Plus" for extra comfort and a 30-day Trip Pass are being tested; see the prices above. Nothing is charged today.

Does "Reserve my spot" charge me?

No. It only saves your interest. We don't take payments on this page.

Will safety features ever be paid?

No. Verification and the safety button stay free.

How do you verify people?

With an ID and a selfie, through a specialist provider. You'll do it before you message, join or post a trip.

What do you do with my data?

We use your email and answers only to contact you about Lumen Travels and to improve it. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Can I join if I'm under 18?

No. Lumen Travels is for people aged 18 and over.
